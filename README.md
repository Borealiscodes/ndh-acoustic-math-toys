
NDH Acoustic Math Toys

Two zero-dependency, blind- and neurodivergent-accessible interactive math toys distilling zero-float Q16.16 fixed-point arithmetic, balanced ternary logic {-1, 0, +1}, 5-cell Unicode Braille (U+2800–U+28FF), and <= 780 Hz pentatonic WebAudio synthesis from .

1. Balanced Ternary & Q16.16 Acoustic Scale (-121 to +121)

Flip five balanced ternary trits (-1, 0, +1) with keys 1–5 (0 resets to equilibrium) to explore base-3 signed weights (81, 27, 9, 3, 1), live Q16.16 hexadecimal representations, 5-cell Unicode Braille bars, and D–E–G–A–B pentatonic intervals.

2. Euclidean & Pentatonic Rhythm Mandala E(k, n)

Distribute k pulses evenly across n steps using W/S (pulses) and A/D (steps), then press Space to hear how integer Euclidean ratios (Bjorklund distribution) generate traditional Irish Bodhrán and Japanese Taiko rhythms below 780 Hz.
